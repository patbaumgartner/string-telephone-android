# Code of Conduct

String Telephone welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Participation should be respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat others with empathy and respect.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy, consent, and differing perspectives.
- Help keep discussions focused and welcoming to newcomers.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, personal
attacks, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.
These standards apply in project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
community spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at
[contact@patbaumgartner.com](mailto:contact@patbaumgartner.com). Do not post
private details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy as far as
possible, and respond proportionately. They may remove inappropriate content,
request changes in behaviour, or restrict participation for repeated or serious
violations.
